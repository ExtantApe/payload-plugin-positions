# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The repo, with the standard Payload plugin kit and an empty plugin. Nothing to install yet. Public since 2026-11-22.
- **Core.** The `position-grants` collection (holder, position, scope kind and id, start and end, granted by, status, revoke stamp, notes), host options for position types and scope kinds, `canGrant`, `canRevoke`, `canRead` and `refuseGrant`, audit hooks before and after grant, revoke and expiry, the `hasPosition` and `positionsOf` helpers, and `expireGrants` for a scheduled sweep. Without a `canGrant` that answers exactly `true`, nobody can grant. For K5225. A unique `liveKey` makes the one-live-grant rule hold under concurrent requests.
- **Review fixes.** Resuming a paused grant, or moving an end date later or to none, now needs `canGrant` (given the existing grant and what is asked) and the veto; `canRevoke` is given `id` and `data`. Audit hooks for pause, resume and end-date change. The holder reaches `canGrant` as a plain id. Self-grants, self-resumes and self-extensions are refused unless `allowSelfGrant`. Grants can't be deleted, even with `overrideAccess`. Notes are readable only by people who pass `canGrant`; notes are capped at 2000 characters and scope ids at 200. Helpers only accept a user from the plugin's users collection. `refuseGrant` is required (`false` for none). New append-only `position-grant-history`.

Tested: 46 tests against a real Payload and an in-memory MongoDB. They cover grant and refusal paths, scope and date validation, duplicates (six simultaneous grants, one wins), a host veto (also for server code, resume and extension), fail-closed access with six wrong kinds of answer, a revoke-only user who can't resume or extend, self-grants both ways, no deletes, notes hidden from holders and length limits, the history, date windows, revoke, pause, final states, expiry and audit order, and refusals through bulk update, REST and GraphQL. Mutation checks (no resume and extend gate, no delete hook, loose `canGrant`) each fail tests.
Not tested: a real app or the admin in a browser; a very large number of grants.
